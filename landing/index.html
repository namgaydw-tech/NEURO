How It Works

From patient selection to clinical decision — in four steps

1

Patient Selection

Select a patient from the database. The system loads their history, previous analyses, and EEG recordings.

2

AI Analysis

Run BrainLat (EEG/clinical) or DARWIN (handwriting). The model processes data, checks for hallucinations, and generates predictions.

3

Clinical Decision

Review predictions, brain region analysis, and recommendations. Generate a report. Book OT if surgery is needed.

4

Track Progression

Every analysis stored in the patient timeline. Compare results over time to track disease progression or treatment response.
